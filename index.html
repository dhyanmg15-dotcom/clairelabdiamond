About Claire Lab Diamond

Claire Lab Diamond supplies lab grown diamonds to jewelers, retailers and manufacturers. Every diamond is made to order in the shape, size and grade you ask for, comes with an IGI certificate, and ships insured worldwide at no extra charge.

This website is our introduction and the quickest way to reach us. Send your requirement and we reply with a trade quote on WhatsApp.

Carat

The weight of the diamond. A higher carat means a larger stone.

Color

Graded from D (colorless) down the alphabet. D, E and F look icy white.

Clarity

How free the stone is from marks. FL and IF are the cleanest; VS and SI show small marks under magnification.

Cut

How well the facets are shaped and polished. A better cut gives more sparkle.
